## Scoring Criteria

- Demand (25%): search volume + trend direction
- Competition inverse (25%): low-competition niches score higher  
- Price potential (15%): higher possible price = higher score
- Buyer intent (20%): does it solve a concrete paying problem?
- Entry difficulty inverse (15%): easier to start = higher score

## Key Findings from Research

- **Affinity Publisher is now FREE FOREVER** since Canva acquisition (Oct 2025) — professional page layout at zero cost
- **Gumroad** is the best starting platform: $0 monthly, 10% per sale, built-in affiliate program
- **Highest-margin products**: Templates (98%+), Prompt Libraries (98%), Digital Planners (95-96%)
- **Reddit marketing**: Value-bombing (500+ word helpful posts WITHOUT links) outperforms direct promotion 10:1
- **Pinterest SEO**: Each pin lives 3-6 months; 5-10 pins per product; 35+ char titles with primary keyword first
- **Optimal product length**: 30-80 pages for guides, 100-200+ for workbooks, 200-500 for planners
