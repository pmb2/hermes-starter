# Video Implementation Notes

## Video 1: "Making a Small Business Website in 2026? Do This." — Steve Builds Websites
Key lessons:
1. **Be HUMAN, not corporate** — Small business sites that try to look like big corporations hurt trust. Show the real person, the real truck, the real story. No stock photos, no corporate jargon.
2. **Don't assume they know** — The customer has never hired a plumber before. Spell out everything: what service, what cost, how fast.
3. **Objection busters on the CALL, not the site** — Address worries (cost, speed, trust) during the sales conversation, not as badges on the customer's website.
4. **Simple > clever** — One message per section. Clean hierarchy. If it confuses, it loses.
5. **Design quality = trust** — Even with good content, bad visuals kill conversions.

## Video 2: "Kimi K3 Designs Websites That Feel Like MOVIES For Just $1" — Nick Saraev
1. **Cinematic storytelling** — Every site should feel like a movie trailer. Scroll-driven narrative, emotional arc, visual impact.
2. **Speed + quality at low cost** — The right tools let you iterate fast.
3. **Single-sentence architecture** — A good prompt should define the entire site.

## Video 3: "How I Built 30 Microsites to Take Over a City" — Jesse Cunningham
1. **Quantity over perfection** — 30 microsites each with 40-50 pages beats 1 perfect site.
2. **Microsites, not monoliths** — One site per plumber, not one site for all.
3. **AI content generation** — Use AI to research, write, and publish at scale.
4. **Rank and rent model** — Build sites that rank, capture leads, sell to businesses.
5. **Suburban focus** — Target less competitive areas first (suburbs around Albany, not the city).
6. **Vertical selection** — Pick niches with high search volume and low competition.
7. **Lead capture > traffic** — The goal isn't visitors, it's phone calls.
8. **Speed of deployment** — "Operate 30 microsites in the time it takes to create one conventional site."
