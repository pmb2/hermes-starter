# BizDev Strategy Questions

Saved from session 2026-06-10 — the operator's strategic approach to C2C contracting
in the MES/Solumina space.

## Core Constraints
- **Direct C2C only** — the company invoices client directly.
- **Subcontracting through layers/SIs IS acceptable** IF they contract on
  **capability** (Solumina/MES expertise, delivery), not individual vetting.
- **Avoid primes directly** (Lockheed, RTX, Boeing, Northrop, L3Harris, GD)
  — too much vendor scrutiny. Only engage through capability-focused SI/partner.
- **No roles requiring** personal background checks, individual ITAR cert,
  or personal security clearance.
- **"Unhireable" positioning** — sell expertise through creative C2C paths.

## Strategic Questions for the operator (Asked, pending discussion)

1. **Offerings** — What specific MES/Solumina service packages should we
   define? (Implementation, integration, upgrade, migration, audit prep,
   training, etc.)

2. **ICPs** — What does the ideal direct C2C client look like? (Company size,
   sector, technology stack, contract value, engagement length)

3. **Channels** — What channels work best for reaching decision makers?
   (LinkedIn, direct email, RFP responses, ExampleVendor partner network,
   SI referrals)

4. **Positioning** — How do we frame the value prop for capability-based
   C2C contracts without triggering individual vetting?

## Outreach Pipeline
1. Research target company
2. Identify decision maker
3. Generate personalized outreach (calibrated voice)
4. Review with the operator before sending
5. Schedule follow-up (suggested: 5 days)
6. Track in BizDev pipeline

## Decision Makers to Target
- Director of MES / Manufacturing IT
- VP of Digital Transformation / Manufacturing Engineering
- Director of Manufacturing Operations
- Procurement Category Manager (contract vehicles)
- Program Manager, MES Integration

## Channels
- Email (Gmail SMTP via bizdev agent — calibrated voice)
- LinkedIn (manual via the operator — no automation without approval)
- ExampleVendor/Solumina partner network referrals
- SAM.gov RFP responses (strategic targeting)

## Key Insight (June 10, 2026)
the operator doesn't need thousands of contacts. He needs focused targeting:
a few well-researched ICPs, specific named decision makers, strategic
offerings with clear value prop, and multiple approach channels.
Build the framework first, then execute.
