# Supplement Certification Hierarchy

Quick reference for evaluating supplement quality claims.

## The Hierarchy (Best → Baseline)

| Badge | Level | What It Means | Used By | Cost Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NSF Certified for Sport** | 🥇 Gold | 270+ banned substance tests + GMP audit + label claim verification. Pro sports standard (NFL, MLB, Olympics). Random retesting. | **Thorne** (most products) | +50-100% vs generic |
| **USP Verified** | 🥇 Gold | USP sets the strictest US purity/potency standards. Audited manufacturing, label accuracy, contamination testing. | Some **Life Extension** products | +30-80% vs generic |
| **Third-Party GMP** (NSF GMP, UL GMP, SGS GMP) | ✅ Strong | Independent auditor inspects the manufacturing facility. Covers sanitation, equipment, personnel, record-keeping. | **NOW Foods, Life Extension**, many | +10-30% vs generic |
| **3rd Party Batch Tested** (HPLC, mass spec) | 🔬 Good | Each production batch tested for identity + purity + potency. Results published or available on request. | **Nootropics Depot** | +5-20% vs generic |
| **Manufacturer GMP** (self-declared) | ⚠️ Baseline | Company claims compliance with FDA cGMP. All US supplement makers are legally required to follow FDA cGMP. Means nothing as a differentiator. | EVERY company | Baseline |
| **"GMP Certified" (no auditor named)** | ❓ Unknown | No verifiable third party. Could mean anything from real certification to a marketing claim. Request proof. | Many Alibaba sellers | Variable |
| **No certification claimed** | ❌ Risk | No way to evaluate quality controls. Buyer assumes all risk. | Unknown | Cheapest |

## Key Facts to Know

- **FDA cGMP is NOT optional** — every US supplement manufacturer is legally required to follow Current Good Manufacturing Practices (21 CFR Part 111). A company saying "we follow GMP" is stating the legal minimum.
- **NSF Certified for Sport is the highest practical cert** because it combines GMP audit + ingredient verification + banned substance screening + label claim testing. It's what professional athletes use.
- **"GMP certified" without an auditor name is suspect** — legitimate GMP certs name the certifying body (NSF, UL, SGS, Intertek, Eurofins).
- **HPLC batch testing is a separate thing from GMP** — a company can have GMP without batch-level HPLC. Nootropics Depot does both.
- **Alibaba CoAs can be faked** — always verify the batch number and contact the testing lab listed on the CoA before trusting. Cross-reference supplier name with CoA lab name.

## How to Verify a Claim

1. **NSF Certified for Sport:** Search `info.nsf.org/Certified/Sport/` — products listed with cert number
2. **USP Verified:** Search `www.usp.org/verified-supplements` — products listed with mark
3. **3P GMP:** Ask for certificate number and auditor name (NSF, UL, SGS). Check directly with the auditor.
4. **Alibaba CoA:** Call the testing lab. Ask if they tested batch [batch number] for [supplier name]. If they can't confirm, CoA is likely fake.
5. **"Tested in-house":** Means nothing — no independent verification. Ask for 3rd party results.
